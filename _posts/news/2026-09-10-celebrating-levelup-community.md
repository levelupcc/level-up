Ten years ago, the LevelUp site relaunched as a community-owned site as its core funding wound down. For many projects, that would have been the likely end. Thanks to a steady stream of volunteer efforts, however, LevelUp continues to grow - both with updated content, improvements to its backend, new community members, and continued activity and engagement.  To celebrate the decade of community support, we have re-launched the [News and Updates](/news) section and filled in highlights of contributions from the community and to better track the next decade of LevelUp as a shared resource for digital safety trainers.
