The digital safety landscape is constantly evolving, and alongside this, the community provides new, updated, and contextually refreshed guides, curricula, and training support materials. While Level Up is focused more on evergreen trainer support, these community resources are critical to the digital safety trainer ecosystem.  In August 2024, we did a full refresh of linked content in the [Community Resources and Tools](https://www.level-up.cc/community/community-resources-and-tools/) section, including a [spreadsheet tracking hundreds of resources by updated date and language localization](https://www.level-up.cc/community/community-resources-and-tools/
), removing outdated content and adding new topics on things like [tabletop exercises and hands-on engagements](https://internews.org/resource/tabletop-exercise-scenarios-and-facilitation-guide-for-digital-safety-training-for-journalists/).
