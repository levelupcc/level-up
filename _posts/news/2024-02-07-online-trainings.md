Since the beginning of the pandemic, the [Access Now Digital Security Helpline](https://www.accessnow.org/help/) has been doing a lot of digital security training online, as surely also with many others in the digital safety trainer community. Through this experience, we have developed new ways to apply the [ADIDS framework](https://www.level-up.cc/before-an-event/levelups-approach-to-adult-learning/#the-adids-approach) in online training engagements.

Although the pandemic lockdowns are now over and people are starting to travel and do in-person training again, we think that online training will continue to be relevant and practiced by many trainers and/or organizations. Based on this thought, we decided to develop documentation to collect our learning on the subject matter, and we imagine that this might be useful for other trainers as well.

In consultation with the awesome Cheekay, we created the following content for Level Up:

* [Using ADIDS for online digital security training](https://level-up.cc/before-an-event/creating-safe-spaces/#how-to-create-a-safe-space-in-online-training
)
* [How to create safe space for online training](https://level-up.cc/before-an-event/preparing-sessions-using-adids/#preparing-sessions-using-adids-in-online-training
), including an agenda template
