Nick Sera-Leyva merged in significant resources fronm The Institute for War & Peace Reporting's resource "[Cyberwomen](https://www.cyber-women.com/): Holistic Digital Security Training Curriculum for Women Human Rights Defenders" to refresh LevelUp content across the board. Nick worked with IWPR to re-structure the curriculum content into the ADIDS format for its final published version, as well as localizing it from Spanish into English.
