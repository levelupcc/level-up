This is an update on the Level Up community's efforts to better link content from the Security Education Companion into the LevelUp website. During our last meeting on the topic in October, we took some time to go through the LevelUp site and identify potential areas for cross-linkage with SEC. 

Evan Summers' (and others!) mapped and linked Security Education Companion resources to Level-Up content. You can see an example of these updates at: [Be a Better Trainer](https://level-up.cc/you-the-trainer/be-a-better-trainer/) and see all the changes in this [pull request](https://github.com/levelupcc/level-up/pull/197)

This helps bring the two resources closer together and take full advantage of the training tools offered by the SEC.
