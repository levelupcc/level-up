### Session Title: What's next for LevelUp, a community-owned platform for global digital safety training resources?

**Session Description**: LevelUp (https://level-up.cc/) is a community-owned platform designed to gather resources for the global digital safety training community. The original content was compiled over 6 years ago, and while much of it is still useful, there are components that need to be updated. As the training space continues to evolve, so do the resources required to facilitate or implement digital safety support. While there are many interested parties who are willing to invest in the maintenance of this community resource, coordination has proven difficult in recent years. This community lab will bring together members of the Internet Freedom community who are passionate and knowledgeable about digital security education and training to develop a clear action plan for refining the LevelUp content. The session will focus on capturing (via online collaboration tools) feedback on the current content/format, mapping the specific skills/interest areas/availability of those interested in contributing, and begin the process of setting up a 6-month plan for implementing the most urgent updates (this process may require additional time and inputs from the community following the session).
